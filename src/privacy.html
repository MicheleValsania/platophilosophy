Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains what personal data is processed when you use platophilosophy.com and, once it is released, the PLATO dialogue app. It has two parts: Part A covers the website and applies now; Part B covers the app, which is not yet available.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Michele Valsania, who runs PLATO as an independent project. For any question about this policy or your data, write to contact@platophilosophy.com.

Part A — The website

A1. What we process and why

  • Visiting the site. Our hosting provider (Netlify) processes technical data such as your IP address, browser and the page requested, in order to deliver the pages and protect the service. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure website.
  • Visitor statistics. We use GoatCounter to count page views. It sets no cookies and does not store your IP address; it uses your IP address and browser only briefly, to estimate your country and avoid counting the same visit twice, and keeps aggregate figures such as pages visited, referring site, browser, screen size and country. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in understanding which pages are useful.
  • Waitlist. If you join the waitlist, we receive your email address through Formspree and manage the mailing list with Sender. We use it to tell you when the app launches and to send occasional updates. Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time through the unsubscribe link in every email or by writing to us.
  • Emails you send us. If you write to us, we use your message and address to reply. Our mailbox is hosted by Infomaniak. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you.
  • Interactive map. The map page loads the Leaflet library from unpkg and map tiles from OpenStreetMap. Your browser therefore connects to these services, which receive your IP address.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising or build profiles.

A2. Cookies and local storage

The website sets no cookies. If you choose a light or dark theme, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage; it stays on your device and is not sent to us.

A3. Service providers and international transfers

  • Netlify (United States): website hosting.
  • GoatCounter: cookieless, aggregate visitor statistics.
  • Formspree (United States): receiving waitlist sign-ups.
  • Sender (European Union): managing the mailing list and sending emails.
  • Infomaniak (Switzerland): email hosting.
  • unpkg and OpenStreetMap (OpenStreetMap Foundation, United Kingdom): software library and map tiles for the interactive map.

Some providers are located outside the European Economic Area. Where this is the case, transfers rely on the safeguards provided by the GDPR, such as an adequacy decision or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

A4. How long we keep data

  • Waitlist: until you unsubscribe, or until we stop sending updates.
  • Emails: as long as needed to handle your request, and no longer than two years after the last exchange.
  • Statistics: aggregate figures, which do not identify you, may be kept indefinitely.
  • Hosting logs: for the limited period set by the hosting provider.

Part B — The PLATO dialogue app

The app is not yet available. This part describes what it is designed to process; we will review it, and inform waitlist members of any change, before the app is released.

B1. What the app will process

  • Account: your email address and, if you choose one, a display name. We never see or store your password in plain text.
  • Conversations: to answer you, the messages of the current conversation are processed on our servers and sent to Anthropic (see B2). Conversations about philosophy may reveal your religious or philosophical beliefs. For this reason we do not keep your dialogues unless you explicitly choose to save them (“Save my dialogues”). This choice is separate from creating an account and can be withdrawn at any time. Without it, a conversation exists only for the duration of your session.
  • Reading path: a short record of where you have been — works and themes discussed, preferred language — used to continue your path from one dialogue to the next. It never records what you believe or how good you are: the app does not grade its users.
  • Credits and purchases: your obol balance and purchase history. Payments are handled by the payment provider; we do not receive or store your card details.
  • Technical data: error reports and technical logs, to keep the service working and secure.
  • Support: messages you send us.

Legal bases: performing our contract with you (account, the conversation in progress, credits and purchases); your explicit consent for saved dialogues (Article 9(2)(a) GDPR); our legitimate interest in security and in keeping the service working. We never use your dialogues for marketing or profiling.

B2. App service providers

  • Anthropic (United States): generating dialogue responses. Your messages are sent to Anthropic's API for this purpose.
  • Hosting, accounts and database: the provider will be named here before release (currently planned: Firebase / Google Cloud).
  • Payments: the payment provider will be named here before purchases are offered (currently planned: Stripe for web payments; Apple, Google and RevenueCat if mobile apps are released).

B3. Retention and deletion

  • Saved dialogues: kept until you delete them. In the app you can delete a single dialogue, all your dialogues or your account, and export your conversations.
  • Inactive accounts: deleted automatically after 24 months without use.
  • Technical logs: 30 days.
  • After deletion: data is removed within 30 days, except where we must keep it by law (for example, purchase records).

B4. Age

The app is intended for users aged 15 and over. Users under 18 may buy obols only with the consent of a parent or guardian, and we never carry out commercial profiling of minors. If you believe that a child under 15 has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it. The website itself can be read by anyone without providing any data.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

  • access the data we hold about you, and receive a copy;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your data;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

Write to contact@platophilosophy.com; we will reply within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of the country where you live or work.

Security

The website is served only over HTTPS, and we use established providers for hosting, email and, in the future, the app. No transmission over the Internet is completely secure, but we limit the data we collect to what each feature needs.

Links to other sites

The website links to external resources, such as the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy or the Perseus Digital Library. Their own privacy policies apply.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and show the date of the latest version at the top. Significant changes affecting waitlist members or app users will also be announced by email or in the app.

In short:
The website sets no cookies and counts visits without tracking you. We only have your email if you join the waitlist or write to us, and you can ask us to delete it at any time. We don't sell your data.
