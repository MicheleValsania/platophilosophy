Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains what personal data is processed when you use platophilosophy.com and the PLATO dialogue app (app.platophilosophy.com). It has two parts: Part A covers the website; Part B covers the app.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Michele Valsania, who runs PLATO as an independent project. For any question about this policy or your data, write to contact@platophilosophy.com.

Part A — The website

A1. What we process and why

  • Visiting the site. Our hosting provider (Netlify) processes technical data such as your IP address, browser and the page requested, in order to deliver the pages and protect the service. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure website.
  • Visitor statistics. We use GoatCounter to count page views. It sets no cookies and does not store your IP address; it uses your IP address and browser only briefly, to estimate your country and avoid counting the same visit twice, and keeps aggregate figures such as pages visited, referring site, browser, screen size and country. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in understanding which pages are useful.
  • Waitlist. If you join the waitlist, you sign up on a form page hosted by Sender, which manages the mailing list and sends our emails. Sender then emails you a link to confirm your address, and records when you signed up and confirmed. We use your address to invite you to the app, to tell you about its launch and to send occasional news about the project. To invite you, we copy your address and language from Sender into the app's list of invited users (see Part B). The sign-up page belongs to Sender, which may use cookies there under its own privacy policy; our own pages set none. Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time through the unsubscribe link in every email or by writing to us.
  • Emails you send us. If you write to us, we use your message and address to reply. Our mailbox is hosted by Infomaniak. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you.
  • Interactive map. The map page loads the Leaflet library from unpkg and map tiles from OpenStreetMap. Your browser therefore connects to these services, which receive your IP address.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising or build profiles.

A2. Cookies and local storage

The website sets no cookies. If you choose a light or dark theme, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage; it stays on your device and is not sent to us.

A3. Service providers and international transfers

  • Netlify (United States): website hosting.
  • GoatCounter: cookieless, aggregate visitor statistics.
  • Sender (European Union): waitlist sign-up page, mailing list and emails.
  • Infomaniak (Switzerland): email hosting.
  • unpkg and OpenStreetMap (OpenStreetMap Foundation, United Kingdom): software library and map tiles for the interactive map.

Some providers are located outside the European Economic Area. Where this is the case, transfers rely on the safeguards provided by the GDPR, such as an adequacy decision or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

A4. How long we keep data

  • Waitlist: until you unsubscribe, or until we stop sending news about the project.
  • Emails: as long as needed to handle your request, and no longer than two years after the last exchange.
  • Statistics: aggregate figures, which do not identify you, may be kept indefinitely.
  • Hosting logs: for the limited period set by the hosting provider.

Part B — The PLATO dialogue app

The app at app.platophilosophy.com is in a closed beta, open to people invited from the waitlist. This part describes what it processes today. Features that are not offered yet, such as saved dialogues, a record of your reading path or payments, are not described here: we will update this policy before introducing them.

B1. What the app processes and why

  • Invitation and account. Your email address and language, copied from the waitlist when we invite you; then your email address, which you use to sign in through a link we send you (there is no password); the language you choose; the date on which you confirmed you are at least 15; the number of free messages you have left. Legal basis: performing our contract with you.
  • Conversations. To answer you, your messages and Plato's replies in the current session are kept on our servers and sent to Anthropic (see B2). They are not saved: a session is deleted automatically two hours after the last message (the removal is normally completed within 24 hours). Conversations about philosophy may reveal your religious or philosophical beliefs. We process them only to answer you: never for advertising or profiling, and they are not used to train AI models. Legal basis: performing our contract with you and your explicit consent, which you give before your first conversation and can withdraw by deleting your account (Article 9(2)(a) GDPR).
  • Reports. If you report an answer, after a notice in the app, we keep that exchange — your question, the answer and any note you add — so that we can review it and improve Plato. Legal basis: your explicit consent, given when you send the report.
  • Technical data. For each message we record your account identifier, the time, the amount of text processed (in tokens), the cost and the outcome, never the text itself. We also keep counters to apply limits, such as messages per hour and daily spending. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in security, preventing abuse and controlling costs.
  • In your browser. The app saves your language, your age confirmation and, while you wait for the sign-in link, your email address in your browser's local storage; the sign-in service keeps your session there too. This is necessary for the app to work and is not used for tracking. The app uses no analytics or advertising tools.

B2. App service providers and international transfers

  • Google (Firebase and Google Cloud): sign-in, database, servers and hosting of the app, and the sign-in emails. The database and servers are in the European Union (Belgium); the sign-in service (Firebase Authentication) may store account data outside the European Union.
  • Anthropic (United States): generates Plato's replies. Your messages are processed outside the European Union, under Anthropic's commercial terms, which do not allow them to be used to train its models.

Transfers outside the European Economic Area rely on the safeguards provided by the GDPR, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

B3. How long we keep data

  • Sessions: deleted automatically two hours after the last message.
  • Reports: 180 days.
  • Technical logs: 30 days.
  • Account: until you delete it. In the app's settings you can delete your account at any time: this immediately removes your account, remaining messages, sessions, reports and technical logs.
  • Daily totals (number of messages and overall cost per day), which do not identify anyone, may be kept longer.

B4. Age

The app is intended for users aged 15 and over; you confirm your age before your first conversation. If you believe that a child under 15 has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it. The website itself can be read by anyone without providing any data.

B5. Answers generated by AI

Plato's replies are generated by an artificial intelligence and can be wrong. They are not professional advice of any kind.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

  • access the data we hold about you, and receive a copy;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your data;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

Write to contact@platophilosophy.com; we will reply within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of the country where you live or work.

Security

The website is served only over HTTPS, and we use established providers for hosting, email and the app. No transmission over the Internet is completely secure, but we limit the data we collect to what each feature needs.

Links to other sites

The website links to external resources, such as the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy or the Perseus Digital Library. Their own privacy policies apply.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and show the date of the latest version at the top. Significant changes will also be announced by email to waitlist members, or in the app.

In short:
The website sets no cookies and counts visits without tracking you. The app keeps conversations only for the session and never saves them. We have your email only if you join the waitlist, use the app or write to us, and you can ask us to delete it at any time. We don't sell your data.
